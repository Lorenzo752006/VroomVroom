?? RECOMMENDED FIRST TEST:
==========================
1. Make sure camera has Target and Target Rigidbody assigned
2. Drive straight at 100+ km/h
   ? Camera should rise and FOV should increase
3. Take a sharp corner
   ? Camera should tilt/bank smoothly
4. Press Left Shift to drift
   ? Camera should pull back with wider FOV
5. Hold Right Mouse Button
   ? Look around, then auto-recenter

??? QUICK TWEAKS (IF NEEDED):
============================

Camera too slow?
? Decrease "Position Smooth Time" (try 0.08)
? Decrease "Rotation Smooth Time" (try 0.10)

Camera too twitchy?
? Increase smooth times (try 0.20-0.25)

Want more dramatic feel?
? Increase "Max Tilt Angle" to 10-12°
? Increase "Look Ahead Amount" to 0.4-0.5
? Increase "Drift FOV Bonus" to 8-10°

Want more realistic feel?
? Decrease "Max Tilt Angle" to 4-5°
? Decrease "Look Ahead Amount" to 0.15-0.2
? Disable "Use Dynamic Height"
