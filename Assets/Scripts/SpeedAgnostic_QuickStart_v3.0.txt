?? SPEED-AGNOSTIC CAMERA - QUICK START v3.0
============================================

? PROBLEM SOLVED: Camera now works at ANY speed - no limits!

WHAT'S DIFFERENT:
=================

? OLD (v2.2): Speed-based thresholds, hardcoded limits
? NEW (v3.0): Physics-based, works at infinite speed

KEY CHANGES:
============

1. ? NO SPEED LIMITS
   - Removed all speed thresholds
   - Removed speed normalization
   - Works at 10 km/h or 10,000 km/h

2. ? TIME-BASED LOOK-AHEAD
   - Old: "Look 15 meters ahead"
   - New: "Look 0.4 seconds ahead"
   - Scales naturally with any velocity

3. ? EXPONENTIAL DAMPING
   - Replaced SmoothDamp (unstable at high speed)
   - New exponential formula (always stable)
   - Framerate-independent

4. ? PROPORTIONAL EFFECTS
   - FOV increases with speed (no cap!)
   - Height increases with speed (self-limiting)
   - All effects scale naturally

NEW SETTINGS:
=============

Position Damping: 0.15
  - Higher = smoother/slower
  - Lower = snappier/faster
  - NOT speed-dependent!

Rotation Damping: 0.2
  - Same concept as position
  - Independent of velocity

FOV:
  - Min FOV: 60° (at rest)
  - Max FOV Increase: 15° (cap: 75° total)
  - FOV Speed Influence: 0.015
    * How much speed affects FOV
    * 0.015 = gentle, 0.030 = aggressive

Look-Ahead:
  - Look Ahead Time: 0.4 seconds
    * "Where will I be in 0.4s?"
    * NOT distance-based!
  - Max Look Ahead Distance: 12m
    * Cap to prevent extremes
    * NOT a speed limit!

Height:
  - Height Speed Factor: 0.2
    * Meters gained per 10 m/s
    * Proportional, self-limiting

Drift:
  - Drift Threshold: 0.2 (ratio, not speed!)
    * sideways/forward velocity ratio
    * Works at any speed

WHAT MAKES IT WORK:
===================

?? Time-Based Physics
   Instead of "look X meters ahead"
   We do "look X seconds ahead"
   ? Scales perfectly with velocity!

?? Proportional Math
   Instead of normalized speed (0-1)
   We use actual velocity values
   ? No caps, no limits!

?? Stable Damping
   Exponential formula:
   lerp(current, target, 1 - exp(-dt/damping))
   ? Never oscillates, any speed!
