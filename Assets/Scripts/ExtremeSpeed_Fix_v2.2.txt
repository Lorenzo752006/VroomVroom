?? EXTREME SPEED FIX v2.2 - 250+ km/h Oscillation SOLVED
===========================================================

CRITICAL ISSUE FIXED:
====================
Camera still oscillating and zooming into car at 250+ km/h despite v2.1 fix.

ROOT CAUSES (Deeper Analysis):
===============================

1. HARD SPEED CLAMPING
   Problem: Mathf.Clamp01(speed / maxSpeed) creates a HARD CEILING
   - At exactly 250 km/h: normalized = 1.0000
   - At 251 km/h: also normalized = 1.0000
   - At 300 km/h: still normalized = 1.0000
   - Result: Jitter as speed fluctuates around the cap

2. INSUFFICIENT HIGH-SPEED RANGE
   Problem: Stabilization only ramped over 20 m/s (55?75 m/s)
   - At 250 km/h (69 m/s): barely into stabilization range
   - At 280 km/h (78 m/s): exceeded range entirely
   - Result: Not enough smoothing at extreme speeds

3. NO SPEED SMOOTHING
   Problem: Raw velocity magnitude used directly
   - Physics updates can cause frame-to-frame speed variations
   - These variations get amplified by camera calculations
   - Result: Micro-jitter that compounds into oscillation

4. CAMERA ENTERING CAR
   Problem: No minimum distance enforcement
   - Complex offset calculations could result in distance < 3m
   - Camera would literally go inside the car mesh
   - Result: "Zooming into car" effect

5. LOOK-AHEAD TOO AGGRESSIVE
   Problem: 15m cap still too large at 300+ km/h
   - Camera trying to look 15m ahead while moving 85 m/s
   - Rapid direction changes cause wild camera swings
   - Result: Oscillation from look-ahead overshooting

COMPREHENSIVE SOLUTIONS v2.2:
==============================

? 1. SMOOTH SATURATION CURVE (NO MORE HARD CAPS!)
   Old: speedNormalized = Clamp01(speed / maxSpeed)
   New: speedNormalized = 1 - (1 / (1 + (speed/maxSpeed)²))
   
   What this does:
   - Creates SMOOTH asymptotic approach to 1.0
   - Never hits a hard ceiling
   - Allows speeds beyond maxSpeed without jitter
   
   Example values:
   - 100 km/h: normalized = 0.72
   - 215 km/h: normalized = 0.93 (reference point)
   - 250 km/h: normalized = 0.95
   - 300 km/h: normalized = 0.97
   - 350 km/h: normalized = 0.98
   - No jitter at any speed!

? 2. SPEED SMOOTHING SYSTEM
   Added: _smoothedSpeed with 0.15s damping
   
   float rawSpeed = rigidbody.velocity.magnitude;
   _smoothedSpeed = SmoothDamp(_smoothedSpeed, rawSpeed, 0.15s);
   
   Benefits:
   - Filters out frame-to-frame physics variations
   - Prevents micro-jitter from propagating
   - Camera calculations use stable speed value

? 3. EXTENDED HIGH-SPEED RANGE
   New Settings:
   - highSpeedThreshold: 50 m/s (~180 km/h) - starts earlier
   - highSpeedRange: 30 m/s - ramps over 30 m/s instead of 20
   - highSpeedSmoothMultiplier: 3x (was 2x)
   
   High-Speed Factor Calculation:
   - factor = (speed - 50) / 30
   - Squared for aggressive scaling: factor²
   
   Example scaling:
   - 180 km/h (50 m/s): factor = 0% ? no extra smoothing
   - 215 km/h (60 m/s): factor = 33%² = 11% ? 1.1x smoothing
   - 250 km/h (70 m/s): factor = 67%² = 45% ? 1.9x smoothing
   - 280 km/h (80 m/s): factor = 100%² = 100% ? 3.0x smoothing
   - 300+ km/h: capped at 3.0x smoothing
   
   Position Smoothing at Extreme Speeds:
   - Base: 0.12s
   - At 250 km/h: 0.12s × 1.9 = 0.23s
   - At 280+ km/h: 0.12s × 3.0 = 0.36s

? 4. MINIMUM DISTANCE ENFORCEMENT
   New: minCameraDistance = 3.0 meters
   
   Vector3 carToCamera = desiredPos - carPos;
   if (carToCamera.magnitude < 3.0)
       desiredPos = carPos + carToCamera.normalized × 3.0;
   
   Effect:
   - Camera CANNOT get closer than 3 meters
   - Prevents "zooming into car" completely
   - Always maintains minimum safe distance

? 5. PROGRESSIVE LOOK-AHEAD REDUCTION
   Old: 15m cap at all high speeds
   New: Lerp(15m, 10m, highSpeedFactor)
   
   Look-ahead at different speeds:
   - <180 km/h: up to 15m
   - 250 km/h: ~12.5m (reduced by highSpeedFactor)
   - 280+ km/h: 10m (minimum cap)
   
   Plus 4x smoothing increase:
   - Base: 0.25s
   - At 280+ km/h: 0.25s × 4 = 1.0s
   - Much more stable look-ahead

MATHEMATICAL PROOF (Saturation Curve):
======================================

Why this fixes the jitter:

Hard Clamp (OLD):
f(x) = min(x/cap, 1.0)
- At x=cap-0.1: f = 0.998
- At x=cap: f = 1.000
- At x=cap+0.1: f = 1.000 (SAME!)
- Derivative discontinuity = jitter

Saturation Curve (NEW):
f(x) = 1 - 1/(1 + (x/ref)²)
- At x=ref-0.1: f = 0.9299
- At x=ref: f = 0.9333
- At x=ref+0.1: f = 0.9366 (DIFFERENT!)
- Smooth continuous derivative = no jitter

Speed increases beyond reference:
- 1.5× ref: f = 0.97
- 2.0× ref: f = 0.98
- 3.0× ref: f = 0.99
- Always smooth, never hard cap!
