FORZA HORIZON STYLE CAR CONTROLLER - SETUP GUIDE
================================================

The CarMovement script is now a fully-featured Forza Horizon-style car controller!

SETUP INSTRUCTIONS:
==================

1. ADD THE SCRIPT TO YOUR CAR:
   - Select your Ferrari car GameObject in the hierarchy
   - Add the CarMovement component (if not already attached)

2. ASSIGN REFERENCES IN INSPECTOR:
   
   Car Configuration:
   - Drag your "Ferrari_Data" asset to the "Car Data" field
   
   Wheel Colliders (Physics):
   - Assign Front Left, Front Right, Rear Left, Rear Right WheelColliders
   
   Wheel Bones (Visuals):
   - Assign the visual wheel meshes (transforms) for all 4 wheels
   
   Visual Settings:
   - If wheels rotate wrong, adjust "Wheel Rotation Offset" (try 0,90,0 or 0,0,90)

3. ADJUST FORZA-STYLE SETTINGS (Optional):
   
   Acceleration Boost (1-3):
   - 1.0 = Realistic simulation
   - 1.6 = Forza Horizon feel (DEFAULT)
   - 2.5+ = Pure arcade
   
   Stability Assist (0-1):
   - 0.0 = No assistance (hardcore sim)
   - 0.5 = Forza Horizon balance (DEFAULT)
   - 1.0 = Maximum stability (easy mode)
   
   Counter Steer Assist (0-1):
   - Helps prevent spinning during slides
   - 0.35 = DEFAULT (Forza-like)
   
   Anti Roll Force (0-10):
   - Prevents body roll during turns
   - 5.0 = DEFAULT (balanced)
   
   Speed Downforce (0-500):
   - Adds grip at high speeds
   - 200 = DEFAULT (good stability)
   
   Assisted Braking:
   - TRUE = Automatic brake when reversing direction (Forza-style)
   
   Drift Friction (0.2-0.8):
   - Lower = easier drifts when handbraking
   - 0.35 = DEFAULT (fun drifting)

CONTROLS:
=========
- W / S (or Up/Down arrows) - Throttle / Brake/Reverse
- A / D (or Left/Right arrows) - Steering
- Left Shift - Handbrake (drift mode)
- Space - (not currently mapped, can add dedicated brake if desired)

TUNING YOUR FERRARI:
===================
Your Ferrari_Data already has great settings! But you can adjust:

For MORE ARCADE feel:
- Increase "Acceleration Boost" to 2.0-2.5
- Increase "Stability Assist" to 0.7-0.9
- Increase "Speed Downforce" to 300-400

For MORE REALISTIC feel:
- Decrease "Acceleration Boost" to 1.2-1.4
- Decrease "Stability Assist" to 0.2-0.3
- Decrease "Speed Downforce" to 100-150

For EASIER DRIFTING:
- Decrease "Drift Friction" to 0.25-0.3
- Decrease "Stability Assist" to 0.2-0.3
- Increase "Counter Steer Assist" to 0.5-0.6

TROUBLESHOOTING:
===============
Car feels too slidey:
- Increase "Stability Assist"
- Increase friction in Ferrari_Data (forwardFrictionStiffness, sidewaysFrictionStiffness)

Car loses traction at high speeds (100+ km/h):
- FIXED in v1.1! Now includes:
  * Dynamic friction that increases with speed
  * Improved downforce at medium-high speeds
  * Load-sensitive grip system
  * Stronger stability control at 100+ km/h
- If still an issue, increase "Speed Downforce" to 250-300
- Increase "Stability Assist" to 0.6-0.7

Car feels too sticky:
- Decrease grip boost values
- Decrease downforce

Wheels look wrong:
- Adjust "Wheel Rotation Offset" (common: 0,90,0 or 0,0,90)

Car accelerates slowly:
- Increase "Acceleration Boost"
- Check Ferrari_Data maxMotorTorque (2800 is good)

Can't drift:
- Make sure handbrake is Left Shift
- Lower "Drift Friction" value
- Lower "Stability Assist"

You can fine-tune these in the Ferrari_Data asset!
